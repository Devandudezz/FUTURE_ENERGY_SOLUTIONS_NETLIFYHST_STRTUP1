Our Story

Built on a Vision of a Greener India

Future Energy Solutions is a licensed solar energy company based in Kollam, Kerala, committed to making clean, reliable, and affordable solar energy accessible to homes, businesses, and institutions.

Though we are a growing company, our team brings together skilled solar professionals, engineers, technicians, and support staff with a shared commitment to delivering high-quality renewable energy solutions. With 50+ successful projects completed and a dedicated team of over 50 professionals, we are steadily building our reputation through quality work and customer satisfaction.

From residential rooftop solar systems to commercial and customised energy solutions, we focus on understanding each customer's requirements and delivering reliable solutions designed for long-term performance.

We are proud to operate as a licensed business and follow the required standards and regulations within the solar energy sector.
