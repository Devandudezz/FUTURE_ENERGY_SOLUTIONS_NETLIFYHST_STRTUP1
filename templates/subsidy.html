Scheme Details

PM Surya Ghar – Full Overview

Everything you need to know about India's biggest rooftop solar subsidy programme.

🏠

Who Can Apply?

Any residential household in India with a valid electricity connection (domestic consumer). One subsidy per household. Apartment residents can also apply for common areas.

⚡

What Do You Get?

Direct bank transfer of Central Govt. subsidy. Up to 300 units of free electricity per month. Net metering — sell excess power back to the grid and earn credits.

🏦

Subsidy Amount

₹30,000/kW for first 2 kW. ₹18,000/kW for the 3rd kW. Maximum of ₹78,000 for systems above 3 kW. Credited directly to your linked bank account within 30 days.

📅

Validity

The scheme runs until 2027 with a target of 1 crore households. Apply early — subsidy is disbursed on first-come-first-served basis through your DISCOM.

📱

Application Portal

Applications via the National Portal for Rooftop Solar (pmsuryaghar.gov.in). We register you, submit the application, and follow up until subsidy is credited.

🔌

Net Metering

After installation, your DISCOM installs a bidirectional net meter. Excess power generated is exported to the grid and credited against your bill every month.

Eligibility Criteria

Are You Eligible for Subsidy?

Check the criteria below. If you meet these conditions, you qualify for the central government subsidy under PM Surya Ghar.

  • Indian residential household with a valid electricity connection
  • Domestic consumer category (not commercial or industrial tariff)
  • System capacity between 1 kW and 10 kW
  • Install only through an MNRE-empanelled vendor (like us)
  • Bank account linked to Aadhaar (for subsidy credit)
  • No previous rooftop solar subsidy availed on the same property
  • Roof should have adequate shadow-free space (approx. 10 sqft per 100W)
